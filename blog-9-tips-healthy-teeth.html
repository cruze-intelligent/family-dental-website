9 tips to have healthy teeth

December 15, 2025 · Family Dental Clinic

Healthy teeth come from small habits repeated every day. Here are nine our team recommends.

1. Floss before you brush

Flossing removes plaque and harmful bacteria from between your teeth. Brushing alone is not enough.

2. Use mouthwash

Together with brushing and flossing, mouthwash helps keep your breath fresh and your confidence high.

3. Chew sugar-free gum

Sugar-free gum after meals can help prevent cavities. Too much sugar, including sweets and chocolate, harms your teeth.

4. Ask about sealants

Sealants protect the chewing surfaces of back teeth, where bacteria like to collect. They work best alongside regular brushing.

5. Use fluoride

Fluoride strengthens tooth enamel. Choose a fluoride toothpaste for the whole family.

6. Quit smoking

Smoking stains and damages teeth and gums, and causes many serious diseases.

7. Visit your dentist regularly

Come in twice a year. Problems caught early are easier and cheaper to fix.

8. Brush twice a day

Morning and night, for at least two minutes. It's essential for healthy teeth and gums.

9. Find a suitable toothbrush

Choose a soft brush that fits your mouth comfortably, or an electric toothbrush, and replace it every three months.

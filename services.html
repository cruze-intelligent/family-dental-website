Questions

About treatment

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on what your teeth need. After your checkup we explain your options and give you the full price before any treatment starts. No hidden costs.

Does treatment hurt?

We use local anaesthesia for procedures that could be uncomfortable, explain every step, and go at your pace. Tell us if you feel nervous and we'll take extra care.

How long do braces take?

Every smile is different. Most braces treatment takes months rather than weeks, with regular review visits. We'll give you an estimate at your consultation.

Can you save a painful tooth instead of removing it?

Often, yes. A filling or root canal treatment can save many painful teeth. We only recommend extraction when a tooth can't be saved.

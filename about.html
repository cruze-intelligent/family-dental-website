We are here for your care.

Family Dental Clinic is a private health facility in Gulu City with a vision to grow into a private not-for-profit dental hospital for Northern Uganda.

Our story

Born from a vision in dental school

"Nine years ago in dental school, I had a dream about a family dental hospital providing free dental services to communities, cultures and languages across the region."

Fast forward to today: Family Dental Clinic is the first step towards realising that dream. We are a modern dental care facility offering comprehensive outpatient services with high standards of quality and patient care.

Alongside everyday treatment, we run affordable dental outreaches and offer free dental consultations for children and the elderly, because good oral health should be within reach of every family.

Our vision

To become a private not-for-profit hospital.

A dental hospital that serves Northern Uganda first and foremost, where cost is never the reason a family goes without care.

Our mission

What we work on every day

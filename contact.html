Questions

Before your first visit

Is the consultation really free for children and the elderly?

Yes. Consultations for children and elderly patients are free. If treatment is needed, we explain the cost before anything starts.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead means a shorter wait. Send us a WhatsApp message and we'll give you a time.

What should I do if I have a toothache right now?

Call or WhatsApp us straight away on +256 775 412 434. We'll do our best to see you the same day.

Will I know the price before treatment?

Always. We offer clear pricing with no hidden costs, and you'll hear the full cost before we begin.

Can I see a doctor as well as a dentist?

Yes. Our medical branch, Family Medical Centre on Juba Road, Kirombe, offers medical, laboratory and ultrasound services every day from 7am to 10pm.
